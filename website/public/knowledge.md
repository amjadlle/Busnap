# Busnap Knowledge Base

## Overview
Busnap is a free and open-source smart proximity alarm app built with Flutter for bus and transit commuters. It wakes you up before reaching your bus or train stop so you never miss your destination.

## Core Features
1. **Multi-Stage Progressive Alarms**:
   - **5 km**: Gentle heads-up notification.
   - **2 km**: Preparation chime & reminder to pack bags.
   - **1 km**: Loud continuous wake-up alarm.
2. **100% Privacy-First & On-Device**:
   - Zero accounts, zero registration, zero ads, and zero telemetry.
   - GPS calculations and route monitoring are computed 100% locally on the device.
3. **Turn-by-Turn Road Route Distance**:
   - Uses OpenStreetMap (Nominatim, Valhalla, and OSRM) to calculate actual road journey distance rather than naive straight-line radius ("as the crow flies").
4. **Battery-Optimized Background GPS**:
   - Uses native Android Foreground Services with distance throttling to keep battery drain under 1% for regular 30-60 min commutes.
5. **Custom Wakeup Alarm Sounds**:
   - Heavy sleeper? Supports custom audio files (MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A) from your device storage.
